# Official Curriculum Source

## Curriculum Information

- **Curriculum:** Palestinian Curriculum
- **Grade:** Grade 8
- **Subject:** Science
- **Selected Lesson:** Lesson 3 – Cell Division (انقسام الخلايا)
- **Selected Pages:** 23–29
- **Language:** Arabic
- **Source Type:** Official Science Textbook

## Selected Case Study

The study uses Lesson 3, "Cell Division" (انقسام الخلايا), from the Palestinian Grade 8 Science curriculum as the selected case study.

The selected material covers pages 23–29 of the textbook and includes curriculum content related to cell division, chromosomes, mitosis, meiosis, and related learning activities and questions.

## Role in the Study

The selected curriculum material serves as the official and authoritative knowledge source for the curriculum data preparation stage of the proposed framework.

It will be used as the source material for preparing structured, machine-readable curriculum content in the subsequent step.

## Source Scope

Only the selected curriculum material from pages 23–29 is included in the current case study.

The original curriculum content is preserved as the reference source throughout the curriculum data preparation process.
