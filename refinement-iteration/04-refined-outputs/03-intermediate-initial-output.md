### 1. Explanations

#### Unit 1: Where Meiosis Occurs and Its Function

- **Meiosis** takes place in specialized **reproductive (sexual) cells**, which are located within the **male and female sex organs** of most animals and plants.
- These reproductive cells undergo meiosis because they are directly responsible for producing sex cells called **gametes**.

#### Unit 2: Definition and Result of Nuclear Division

- **Meiosis** is defined as a process of **nuclear division** that transforms a single parent reproductive cell.
- As this nuclear division takes place, **one parent reproductive cell** divides to produce **four daughter cells**, which are known as **gametes**.

#### Unit 3: Chromosome-Number Reduction

- During meiosis, chromosome numbers are halved so that each resulting **gamete** contains **half the chromosome number** of the original parent cell.
- In humans, a parent reproductive cell carries **46 chromosomes**; following meiosis, each produced **gamete** contains **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 4: Quantitative Progression of Meiosis

- The step-by-step numerical progression connects cell quantity with chromosome counts across three clear stages:
  1. **Starting stage:** **1 parent reproductive cell** with **46 chromosomes**.
  2. **Intermediate stage:** Division into **2 intermediate cells**, each carrying **23 chromosomes**.
  3. **Final stage:** Completion of division resulting in **4 gametes**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 5: Maintaining Chromosome Stability Across Generations

- By systematically halving the chromosome count from parent cells to gametes, meiosis plays a direct role in maintaining **chromosome-number stability** across generations in a species.

### 2. Key Vocabulary

- **Meiosis:** The process of nuclear division in a parent reproductive cell that produces four daughter cells (gametes), each having half the original chromosome number.
- **Reproductive (Sexual) Cells:** Cells located in the male and female sex organs of plants and animals that are responsible for producing gametes through meiosis.
- **Gametes:** The four daughter cells created by meiosis, each carrying half the chromosome count of the parent cell (23 chromosomes in humans).
- **Chromosome-Number Reduction:** The process during meiosis where the chromosome count is reduced by half from the parent cell (46 in humans) to each gamete (23 in humans).
- **Chromosome-Number Stability:** The maintenance of a constant chromosome number across generations, which is supported by the halving of chromosomes during meiosis.
