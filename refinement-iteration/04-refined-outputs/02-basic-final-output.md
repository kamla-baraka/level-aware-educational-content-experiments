## Generated Output

### 1. Explanations

#### Unit 1: Where Meiosis Occurs

- **Meiosis** occurs in **reproductive (sexual) cells**.
- These cells are located in the **male and female sex organs** of most animals and plants.
- **Reproductive cells** are responsible for producing **gametes**.

#### Unit 2: What Meiosis Is and What It Produces

- **Meiosis** is defined as the **nuclear division** of a parent reproductive cell.
- During this process, **one parent reproductive cell** divides to produce **four daughter cells**.
- These four resulting daughter cells are called **gametes**.

#### Unit 3: Chromosome-Number Reduction

- Each **gamete** receives **half the chromosome number** of the parent cell.
- In humans, a parent reproductive cell contains **46 chromosomes**.
- After meiosis, each human **gamete** contains **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 4: Quantitative Progression of Cell Division

- The step-by-step numerical progression of meiosis occurs in a clear sequence:
  1. **Starting cell:** **1 parent cell** containing **46 chromosomes**.
  2. **Intermediate stage:** **2 cells**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.
  3. **Final result:** **4 gametes**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 5: Maintaining Chromosome Stability

- **Meiosis** contributes to maintaining **chromosome-number stability** across generations.

---

### 2. Key Vocabulary

- **Meiosis:** The nuclear division of a parent reproductive cell that produces four daughter cells called gametes.
- **Reproductive (Sexual) Cells:** Cells in the male and female sex organs of plants and animals responsible for producing gametes.
- **Gametes:** The four daughter cells produced by meiosis, each having half the chromosome number of the parent cell.
- **Chromosome Reduction:** Having each gamete receive half the original chromosome number of the parent cell (in humans, 46 → 23).
- **Chromosome-Number Stability:** The maintenance of constant chromosome numbers across generations, which meiosis contributes to.
