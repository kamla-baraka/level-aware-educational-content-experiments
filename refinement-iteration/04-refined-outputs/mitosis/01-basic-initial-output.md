## 1. Explanations

### Purpose of Mitosis

- **Definition and Goal**: Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي) is a process of cell division where a cell divides to produce two identical daughter cells.
- **Reproduction in Unicellular Organisms**: Single-celled organisms, such as **Paramecium** (البراميسيوم), use mitosis for reproduction to produce new individuals.
- **Growth and Repair in Humans**: Multicellular organisms, such as humans, use mitosis for **growth** and for **replacing damaged cells**.

### Cell Structures Related to Mitosis

- **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Long, thin, tangled threads of genetic material present inside the nucleus before cell division begins.
- **Duplicated Chromosomes and Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: After DNA duplication, each chromosome consists of two identical strands called sister chromatids.
- **Centromere (سنترومير)**: The central attachment point that holds two sister chromatids together.
- **Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Structures present in animal cells that help form spindle fibers.
- **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل)**: Fine fibers that attach to the centromere of each chromosome to pull chromatids toward opposite sides of the cell.

### Stages of Mitosis in Sequence

Mitosis and cell division follow a fixed order of stages:

1. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**
   - The cell grows and increases in size.
   - **DNA is duplicated** so that genetic material is copied before division.

2. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**
   - Chromosomes condense and become visible as short, thick, duplicated structures.
   - The **nuclear envelope** (الغلاف النووي) and **nucleolus** (النوية) disappear.

3. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**
   - Duplicated chromosomes arrange single file across the **middle of the cell**.
   - **Spindle fibers** attach to the **centromere** of each chromosome from opposite poles.

4. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**
   - **Sister chromatids separate** from each other as spindle fibers contract.
   - Separated chromatids move toward opposite poles of the cell.

5. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**
   - Chromosomes reach the poles and gather in the middle of each newly forming cell region.
   - A new **nuclear envelope** begins to re-form around the chromosomes at each pole.

6. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**
   - The cytoplasm divides to complete cell division.
   - **Difference between Animal and Plant Cells**:
     - In **animal cells**, cytokinesis occurs through **membrane pinching / constriction** (تخصر الغشاء).
     - In **plant cells**, cytokinesis occurs through the formation of a **cell plate / middle lamella** (تكون الصفيحة الوسطى / أكياس غشائية).

### Final Result of Mitosis

- **Daughter Cell Count**: Division results in **two identical daughter cells**.
- **Chromosome Number**: Each daughter cell contains the **exact same chromosome number** as the parent cell.
- **Human Cells**: Human body cells contain **46 chromosomes**; after mitosis, each daughter cell also has **46 chromosomes**.

## 2. Key Vocabulary

1. **Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي)**: Cell division in body cells resulting in two identical daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
2. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**: The stage preceding division where the cell grows and duplicates its DNA.
3. **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Long, thin, tangled threads of genetic material inside the nucleus before chromosomes condense.
4. **Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: Two identical copies of a duplicated chromosome connected together.
5. **Centromere (سنترومير)**: The connection point holding two sister chromatids together.
6. **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل)**: Fibers that attach to centromeres to move and separate chromatids during division.
7. **Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Structures in animal cells that participate in forming spindle fibers.
8. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**: First stage of mitosis where chromosomes appear short and thick, while the nuclear envelope and nucleolus disappear.
9. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**: Second stage of mitosis where duplicated chromosomes line up in the middle of the cell.
10. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**: Third stage of mitosis where sister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles.
11. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**: Fourth stage of mitosis where chromosomes gather at the poles and nuclear envelopes re-form.
12. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**: Division of the cytoplasm that splits the cell into two complete cells.

## 4. Self-Assessment Questions

1. **What is the main purpose of mitosis in human body cells?**
   - **Answer:** Growth and replacing damaged cells.

2. **What critical process occurs regarding DNA during the Interphase (preparatory phase)?**
   - **Answer:** DNA duplication (تضاعف DNA).

3. **In which stage of mitosis do duplicated chromosomes align single file in the middle of the cell?**
   - **Answer:** Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي).

4. **How does cytokinesis differ between animal cells and plant cells?**
   - **Answer:** Animal cells divide by membrane pinching (تخصر الغشاء), whereas plant cells divide by forming a cell plate / middle lamella (تكون الصفيحة الوسطى).

5. **If a human cell with 46 chromosomes divides by mitosis, how many daughter cells are produced, and how many chromosomes does each daughter cell contain?**
   - **Answer:** Two identical daughter cells are produced, and each contains 46 chromosomes.
