## 1. Explanations

### Purpose of Mitosis and Organism Types

- **Definition and Core Objective**: Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي) is a process of cell division that produces two identical daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
- **Reproduction in Unicellular Organisms**: In single-celled organisms such as *Paramecium* (البراميسيوم), mitosis is related to reproduction.
- **Growth and Replacement of Cells in Humans**: In multicellular organisms such as humans, mitosis is related to growth and replacement of cells.
- **Chromosome Number Result**: When human body cells undergo mitosis, the result is two identical daughter cells, each maintaining the same chromosome number of 46.

### Cell Structures Related to Mitosis

- **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Before cell division begins, genetic material inside the nucleus exists as a chromatin network.
- **Duplicated Chromosomes and Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: Before division, DNA is duplicated, forming chromosomes composed of sister chromatids.
- **Centromere (سنترومير)**: Sister chromatids are connected at the centromere, which is also associated with spindle fibers during cell division.
- **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل) and Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Spindle fibers are fine structures associated with the centromere of each chromosome, and centrioles are structures present in animal cells associated with the division process.

### Stages of Mitosis in Sequence

Mitosis progresses through a fixed sequence of stages:

1. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**
   - During this phase before division, **DNA is duplicated**.

2. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**
   - Chromosomes become visible.
   - Concurrently, both the **nuclear envelope** (الغلاف النووي) and the **nucleolus** (النوية) disappear.

3. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**
   - Duplicated chromosomes are arranged in the **middle of the cell**.
   - **Spindle fibers** are associated with the **centromere** of each chromosome.

4. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**
   - **Sister chromatids separate** and move toward opposite poles.

5. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**
   - Chromosomes reach the poles.
   - Nuclear structures re-form around the chromosomes.

6. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**
   - Cytokinesis completes division of the cell.
   - **Comparison of Cell Types**:
     - In **animal cells**, cytokinesis occurs through **membrane pinching / constriction** (تخصر الغشاء).
     - In **plant cells**, cytokinesis occurs through the formation of a **cell plate / middle lamella** (تكون الصفيحة الوسطى).

### Final Result of Mitosis

- **Daughter Cells and Chromosome Number**: Mitosis produces **two identical daughter cells** with the **same chromosome number as the parent cell**.
- **Human Body Cells**: In humans, where the chromosome number is **46**, mitosis results in daughter cells that each contain **46 chromosomes**.

## 2. Key Vocabulary

1. **Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي)**: Cell division resulting in two identical daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
2. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**: The stage before mitosis where DNA is duplicated.
3. **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Genetic material inside the nucleus before chromosomes become visible.
4. **Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: Two identical strands of a duplicated chromosome connected at the centromere.
5. **Centromere (سنترومير)**: The connection point holding sister chromatids together and associated with spindle fibers.
6. **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل)**: Fibers associated with the centromere during cell division.
7. **Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Structures present in animal cells associated with the cell division process.
8. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**: Stage where chromosomes become visible and the nuclear envelope and nucleolus disappear.
9. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**: Stage where chromosomes are arranged in the middle of the cell and spindle fibers are associated with the centromere.
10. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**: Stage where sister chromatids separate and move toward opposite poles.
11. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**: Stage where chromosomes reach the poles and nuclear structures re-form.
12. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**: The process that completes division of the cell following nuclear division.

## 4. Self-Assessment Questions

### 1. Connecting Interphase to the Final Result

**Question:** What happens to DNA during Interphase, and how does the chromosome number in the resulting daughter cells compare to the parent cell?

**Answer:** During Interphase, DNA is duplicated. After division, each daughter cell has the same chromosome number as the parent cell.

### 2. Connecting Structures to Stages

**Question:** What structure is associated with the centromere during Metaphase, and what happens to sister chromatids during Anaphase?

**Answer:** During Metaphase, spindle fibers are associated with the centromere. During Anaphase, sister chromatids separate and move toward opposite poles.

### 3. Connecting Cell Type to Cytokinesis

**Question:** How does cytokinesis differ between animal cells and plant cells when completing cell division?

**Answer:** In animal cells, cytokinesis occurs through membrane pinching (constriction), whereas in plant cells, it occurs through the formation of a cell plate (middle lamella).

### 4. Connecting Organism Type to Purpose

**Question:** State the purpose of mitosis in *Paramecium* compared to its purpose in human body cells.

**Answer:** In *Paramecium*, mitosis is related to reproduction, whereas in human body cells, mitosis is related to growth and replacement of cells.
