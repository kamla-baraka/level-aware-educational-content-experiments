## 1. Explanations

### Purpose of Mitosis and Biological Context

- **Definition and Core Function**: Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي) is a process of cell division that divides a parent cell to produce two identical daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
- **Functional Roles across Organism Types**:
  - In single-celled organisms, such as *Paramecium* (البراميسيوم), mitosis is related to reproduction.
  - In multicellular organisms, such as humans, mitosis is related to growth and replacement of cells.
- **Human Chromosome Number**: In human body cells, the chromosome number is 46. Mitosis results in two identical daughter cells, each containing 46 chromosomes.

### Structural Components Involved in Mitosis

- **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Before cell division, genetic material inside the nucleus exists as a chromatin network.
- **Duplicated Chromosomes and Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: Before division, DNA is duplicated, forming chromosomes composed of sister chromatids.
- **Centromere (سنترومير)**: Sister chromatids are connected at the centromere, which is associated with spindle fibers during cell division.
- **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل) and Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Spindle fibers are fine structures associated with the centromere of each chromosome during division, while centrioles are structures present in animal cells associated with the cell division process.

### Sequential Dynamics of Mitosis and Cell Division

Cell division progresses through a fixed sequence of stages:

1. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**
   - During Interphase before division begins, **DNA is duplicated**.

2. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**
   - Chromosomes become visible.
   - Concurrently, the **nuclear envelope** (الغلاف النووي) and **nucleolus** (النوية) disappear.

3. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**
   - Duplicated chromosomes are arranged in the **middle of the cell**.
   - **Spindle fibers** are associated with the **centromere** of each chromosome.

4. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**
   - **Sister chromatids separate** and move toward opposite poles.

5. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**
   - Chromosomes reach the poles.
   - Nuclear structures re-form around the chromosomes.

6. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**
   - Cytokinesis completes division of the cell.
   - **Divergence by Cell Type**:
     - In **animal cells**, cytokinesis occurs through **membrane pinching / constriction** (تخصر الغشاء).
     - In **plant cells**, cytokinesis occurs through the formation of a **cell plate / middle lamella** (تكون الصفيحة الوسطى).

### Quantitative Outcome of Mitosis

- **Cellular and Chromosomal Output**: Mitosis produces **two identical daughter cells** with the **same chromosome number as the parent cell**.
- **Human Body Cell Output**: In humans, where the chromosome number is **46**, mitosis results in two identical daughter cells, each containing **46 chromosomes**.

## 2. Key Vocabulary

1. **Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي)**: Cell division resulting in two identical daughter cells with the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
2. **Interphase / Preparatory Phase (الطور البيني / التحضيري)**: The stage before division where DNA is duplicated.
3. **Chromatin Network (شبكة كروماتينية)**: Genetic material inside the nucleus before chromosomes become visible.
4. **Sister Chromatids (كروماتيدات شقيقة)**: Two identical strands of a duplicated chromosome connected at the centromere.
5. **Centromere (سنترومير)**: Connection point holding sister chromatids together and associated with spindle fibers during cell division.
6. **Spindle Fibers (خيوط المغزل)**: Fibers associated with the centromere during cell division.
7. **Centrioles (مريكزات)**: Structures present in animal cells associated with the cell division process.
8. **Prophase (الدور التمهيدي)**: Stage where chromosomes become visible and the nuclear envelope and nucleolus disappear.
9. **Metaphase (الدور الاستوائي)**: Stage where chromosomes are arranged in the middle of the cell and spindle fibers are associated with the centromere.
10. **Anaphase (الدور الانفصالي)**: Stage where sister chromatids separate and move toward opposite poles.
11. **Telophase (الدور النهائي)**: Stage where chromosomes reach the poles and nuclear structures re-form.
12. **Cytokinesis (انقسام السيتوبلازم)**: The process that completes division of the cell following nuclear division.
