## Generated Output

### 1. Explanations

#### Unit 1: Where Meiosis Occurs and Its Role

- **Meiosis** occurs in **reproductive (sexual) cells**, which are located in the **male and female sex organs** of most animals and plants.
- These **reproductive cells** are responsible for producing sex cells called **gametes**.

#### Unit 2: Definition and Result of Nuclear Division

- **Meiosis** is defined as the **nuclear division** of a parent reproductive cell.
- During this process, **one parent reproductive cell** divides to produce **four daughter cells**, which are called **gametes**.

#### Unit 3: Chromosome-Number Reduction

- During meiosis, chromosome numbers are reduced so that each resulting **gamete** contains **half the chromosome number** of the original parent cell.
- In humans, a parent reproductive cell contains **46 chromosomes**; after meiosis, each produced **gamete** contains **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 4: Quantitative Progression of Cell Division

- The step-by-step numerical progression connects cell quantity with chromosome counts across three sequential stages:
  1. **Starting stage:** **1 parent reproductive cell** with **46 chromosomes**.
  2. **Intermediate stage:** Division into **2 cells**, each carrying **23 chromosomes**.
  3. **Final stage:** Division completing with **4 gametes**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.

#### Unit 5: Maintaining Chromosome Stability Across Generations

- Each gamete receives half the chromosome number of the parent cell, and **meiosis contributes to maintaining chromosome-number stability across generations**.

### 2. Key Vocabulary

- **Meiosis:** The nuclear division of a parent reproductive cell that produces four daughter cells called gametes, each having half the original chromosome number.
- **Reproductive (Sexual) Cells:** Cells located in the male and female sex organs of plants and animals that are responsible for producing gametes.
- **Gametes:** The four daughter cells produced by meiosis, each carrying half the chromosome count of the parent cell (23 chromosomes in human gametes).
- **Chromosome-Number Reduction:** The process in meiosis where each gamete receives half the chromosome number of the parent cell (in humans, 46 → 23 chromosomes).
- **Chromosome-Number Stability:** The maintenance of constant chromosome numbers across generations, which meiosis contributes to.
