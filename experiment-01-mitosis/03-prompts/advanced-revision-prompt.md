## Original Revision Prompt

Review the generated Advanced Level – Mitosis package strictly against the fixed retrieved curriculum knowledge established earlier.

The goal is to preserve the Advanced Pedagogical Profile and its higher cognitive demand while removing any scientific fact, mechanism, structure, or assumption that was not contained in the fixed retrieved curriculum knowledge.

Please revise the complete Advanced package according to these rules:

- Maintain Apply and Analyze cognitive demand through comparison, interpretation, prediction, justification, and relationships that can be directly supported by the fixed retrieved knowledge.
- Do not introduce new biological mechanisms or structures simply to make the lesson more advanced.
- Remove unsupported information such as protein aggregates in plant cells or any other information not included in the fixed retrieved knowledge.
- Do not provide an external scientific explanation for why plant and animal cytokinesis differ unless that explanation was included in the fixed retrieved knowledge.
- Avoid causal explanations about nuclear-envelope disappearance or chromosome alignment unless the relationship can be directly derived from the fixed retrieved knowledge.
- Hypothetical scenarios may be used only when their answer can be reasoned directly from an explicitly stated relationship in the fixed retrieved knowledge.
- Correct any internally inconsistent example, such as referring to a liver cell in a skin wound.

Keep all experimental constants unchanged:

- Same curriculum source.
- Same selected Mitosis scope.
- Same five Learning Objectives.
- Same fixed retrieved curriculum knowledge.
- Same Advanced Pedagogical Profile.
- Exactly the same four output components:
  1. Explanations
  2. Key Vocabulary
  3. Exercises
  4. Self-Assessment Questions

Do not simplify the package to the Intermediate level. The challenge is to achieve greater reasoning depth using the same curriculum knowledge, not by adding new knowledge.

Return the complete revised Advanced package only.
