# Source Information

## Experiment
Experiment 1 – Mitosis

## Curriculum Source
Palestinian Grade 8 Science Curriculum

## Source Lesson
Cell Division (انقسام الخلايا)

## Source Format
PDF – 7 pages

## Generation Environment
Google NotebookLM

## Source Used in the Experiment
The complete seven-page Cell Division lesson from the Palestinian Grade 8 Science curriculum was uploaded to Google NotebookLM as the curriculum source for this experiment.

## Selected Experimental Lesson
Mitosis (الانقسام المتساوي)

## Experimental Scope
The experiment focused on curriculum content relevant to mitosis, including:

- Preparatory/interphase information relevant to cell division
- Purpose of mitosis
- Chromosome-related concepts required for understanding mitosis
- Prophase
- Metaphase
- Anaphase
- Telophase
- Cytokinesis
- Final result of mitosis
- Relevant information on animal and plant cell division

## Excluded Content
Although the uploaded source also contained other topics within the Cell Division lesson, the following content was outside the scope of Experiment 1:

- Meiosis
- Down syndrome
- Meiotic abnormalities

These topics were not intended to be used in the generation of the Mitosis educational packages.

## Source-Control Note
The uploaded curriculum material served as the authoritative knowledge source for the experiment. Generated educational content was intended to remain within the defined Mitosis scope and the curriculum-grounded knowledge extracted from this source.
