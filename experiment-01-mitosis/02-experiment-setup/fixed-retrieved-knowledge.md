# Fixed Retrieved Curriculum Knowledge

## Experiment
Experiment 1 – Mitosis

## Purpose

Before generating the Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced educational packages, curriculum knowledge relevant to the five fixed learning objectives was retrieved from the uploaded Grade 8 Science source.

This retrieved knowledge was then fixed and used as the common knowledge boundary for all three learner levels.

## LO1 – Purpose of Mitosis

The retrieved curriculum knowledge included:

- In Paramecium, cell division serves as a means of reproduction.
- In humans, mitosis contributes to growth.
- Mitosis also contributes to replacing damaged cells.

## LO2 – Sequence of Mitosis

The fixed sequence used in the experiment was:

1. Interphase
2. Prophase
3. Metaphase
4. Anaphase
5. Telophase
6. Cytokinesis

## LO3 – Main Changes During Mitosis

The retrieved curriculum knowledge included the following changes:

### Interphase
- The cell prepares for division.
- DNA is duplicated before division.

### Prophase
- Chromosomes become visible.
- The nuclear envelope and nucleolus disappear.
- Spindle fibers begin to form.

### Metaphase
- Chromosomes align in the middle of the cell.
- Spindle fibers are associated with chromosomes at the centromere.

### Anaphase
- Sister chromatids separate.
- The separated chromatids move toward opposite poles of the cell.

### Telophase
- Chromosomes reach opposite poles.
- New nuclei form.

### Cytokinesis
- The cytoplasm divides to form two daughter cells.
- Cytokinesis differs between animal and plant cells as presented in the curriculum source.

## LO4 – Final Result of Mitosis

The retrieved curriculum knowledge established that:

- Mitosis produces two daughter cells.
- The daughter cells have the same chromosome number as the parent cell.
- In humans, somatic cells contain 46 chromosomes.

## LO5 – Relevant Cellular Structures

The fixed curriculum knowledge included the following structures relevant to mitosis:

- Chromatin
- Duplicated chromosomes
- Sister chromatids
- Centromere
- Spindle fibers
- Centrioles

## Knowledge Boundary

This fixed retrieved knowledge was used for the Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced generation conditions.

The learner level was not intended to change the scientific knowledge available to the model. Level differentiation was intended to change how this same knowledge was explained, structured, practiced, and assessed.

No external or higher-grade scientific knowledge was intended to be introduced during level-aware generation.
