## 1. Explanations

### Location and Target Cells of Meiosis

Meiosis (الانقسام المنصف) is a specialized nuclear division process that occurs exclusively within **reproductive or sexual cells (الخلايا التناسلية / الجنسية)**. These cells are located within the **male and female sex organs** of most plants and animals.

### Process and Gamete Output

During meiosis, the nucleus of one parent reproductive cell divides. This process results in the production of **four daughter cells**, which are called **gametes (غاميتات)**.

### Chromosome Number Reduction

An important relationship in meiosis is the reduction of the chromosome number to half the original number.

In the human example:

- The parent reproductive cell contains **46 chromosomes**.
- The resulting cells contain **23 chromosomes each**.
- Therefore, each gamete contains half the chromosome number of the original parent reproductive cell.

### Quantitative Progression of Meiosis

The curriculum presents the following quantitative progression:

1. **1 parent reproductive cell** containing **46 chromosomes**.
2. **2 cells**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.
3. **4 final gametes**, each containing **23 chromosomes**.

This progression shows both the increase in the number of cells and the reduction of the chromosome number per cell from 46 to 23.

### Biological Significance

Meiosis contributes to maintaining the **constancy and stability of chromosome numbers** in the cells of a given species across generations.

## 2. Key Vocabulary

- **Meiosis (الانقسام المنصف)**: A nuclear division process occurring in reproductive cells that produces four daughter cells called gametes.
- **Reproductive / Sexual Cells (الخلايا التناسلية / الجنسية)**: Cells located in the male and female sex organs of most animals and plants that undergo meiosis.
- **Gametes (الغاميتات)**: The four daughter cells produced by meiosis.
- **Chromosome Number Reduction (اختزال عدد الكروموسومات)**: The reduction of the chromosome number in each resulting gamete to half the number found in the parent reproductive cell.
- **Chromosome Stability (ثبات عدد الكروموسومات)**: The constancy and stability of chromosome numbers in the cells of a species across generations.
