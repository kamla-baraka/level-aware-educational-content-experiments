## Selected Experimental Lesson

Meiosis (الانقسام المنصف)

## Experimental Scope

The experiment focused only on the curriculum content relevant to Meiosis, including:

- Reproductive / sexual cells
- Location of reproductive cells in the male and female sex organs of most animals and plants
- Meiosis as a nuclear division process in a parent reproductive cell
- Production of four daughter cells called gametes
- Reduction of chromosome number to half the original number
- The human numerical example of 46 chromosomes in the parent cell and 23 chromosomes in the resulting cells / gametes
- The quantitative progression presented in the curriculum:
  - 1 parent cell with 46 chromosomes
  - 2 cells with 23 chromosomes each
  - 4 gametes with 23 chromosomes each
- Biological significance of meiosis in contributing to the constancy and stability of chromosome numbers across generations

## Excluded Content

Although the uploaded source contained additional Cell Division topics, the following content was explicitly excluded from Experiment 2:

- Mitosis
- Down Syndrome
- Meiotic errors / non-disjunction
- Meiosis I and Meiosis II
- Named meiotic stages such as Prophase I and Metaphase I
- Crossing over
- Homologous chromosome pairing
- Genetic variation
- Fertilization
- Other mechanisms or scientific information not explicitly presented within the fixed Meiosis scope

## Source-Control Note
