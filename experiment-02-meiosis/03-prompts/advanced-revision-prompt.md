## Original Revision Prompt

Revise the Advanced-Level Meiosis Educational Package while preserving the fixed Advanced Pedagogical Profile and exactly the same four-component Output Structure.

The current version successfully increases cognitive demand, but some analytical tasks go beyond the Fixed Retrieved Curriculum Knowledge.

Make the following corrections:

Remove the hypothetical scenario asking what would happen if chromosome reduction failed.

Remove any claim that chromosome reduction prevents chromosome numbers from “doubling uncontrollably” or any other causal mechanism not contained in the fixed knowledge.

Remove the calculation and interpretation of the total chromosome sum across all four gametes (4 × 23 = 92). The fixed curriculum knowledge concerns chromosome number per cell, not a combined chromosome total across separate cells.

Do not infer mechanisms explaining how chromosome stability is maintained beyond the fixed curriculum statement that meiosis contributes to maintaining the constancy and stability of chromosome numbers across generations.

Avoid introducing new functional claims such as “primary functions” unless they directly restate the fixed knowledge.

Preserve the Advanced level by creating analytical demand only through relationships already present in the fixed knowledge. You may require the learner to:

- analyze the fixed numerical progression 1 parent cell with 46 chromosomes → 2 cells with 23 chromosomes each → 4 gametes with 23 chromosomes each;
- compare the parent reproductive cell and the resulting gametes;
- interpret fixed curriculum data to identify meiosis;
- connect location, cell output, chromosome reduction, and biological significance;
- justify a conclusion using multiple pieces of evidence explicitly contained in the fixed knowledge;
- synthesize the fixed concepts into an integrated explanation.

Do not add external scientific knowledge, hypothetical chromosome counts, fertilization, Meiosis I/II, named meiotic stages, crossing over, homologous chromosomes, genetic variation, DNA mechanisms, Down Syndrome, meiotic errors, or Mitosis.

Every answer must be fully derivable from the Fixed Retrieved Curriculum Knowledge.

Retain exactly:

1. Explanations
2. Key Vocabulary
3. Exercises
4. Self-Assessment Questions

Maintain Apply and Analyze, low scaffolding, higher learner independence, and deeper integration of the same fixed concepts.

Produce the complete revised Advanced package only.
