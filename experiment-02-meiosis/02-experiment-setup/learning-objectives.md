# Learning Objectives

## Experiment

Experiment 2 – Meiosis

## Selected Lesson

Meiosis (الانقسام المنصف)

## Fixed Learning Objectives

The following five learning objectives were defined before level-aware content generation and were kept fixed across the Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:

1. Identify the location of meiosis in living organisms by specifying that it occurs exclusively within reproductive (sexual) cells in the male and female sex organs of plants and animals.

2. Define meiosis as a nuclear division process occurring in a parent reproductive cell that yields four daughter cells known as gametes.

3. Demonstrate chromosome reduction during meiosis, showing that each generated gamete contains half the original number of chromosomes present in the parent cell, such as 46 chromosomes in the parent cell and 23 chromosomes in the resulting gametes in the human example.

4. Outline the quantitative results of meiosis in terms of the total number of daughter cells produced (4 gametes) and their chromosome counts.

5. Explain the biological significance of meiosis in maintaining the constancy and stability of chromosome numbers in the cells of a given species across generations.

## Experimental Control

The five learning objectives were held constant across all three learner levels:

- Basic
- Intermediate
- Advanced

The scientific learning goals were not changed according to learner level.

Instead, differentiation was introduced through the Pedagogical Profile, which controlled how the same curriculum-grounded knowledge was explained, structured, practiced, and assessed.

## Scope Boundary

The learning objectives were restricted to the fixed Meiosis curriculum scope.

They did not include:

- Down Syndrome
- Meiotic errors / non-disjunction
- Mitosis
- Detailed meiotic stages or mechanisms not explicitly presented in the selected curriculum scope
